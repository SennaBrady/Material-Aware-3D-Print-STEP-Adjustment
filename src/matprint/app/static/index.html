Calibrate a material on your printer

The built-in values are typical figures. A test coupon measured with calipers gives the shrinkage and hole offset of your printer and filament, and replaces them.

1 Make a test coupon

  1. Print it with all slicer compensation off: Shrinkage 100%, X-Y hole and contour compensation 0, auto circle compensation off.
  2. Let it cool completely.
  3. Measure the square across X and across Y, and each hole, with calipers.
